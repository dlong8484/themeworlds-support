Theme Worlds

Support

Theme Worlds is a calm word game for iPhone: swipe a wheel of letters to fill themed crosswords across 27 hand-painted worlds. No timers, no lives, no ads.

Frequently asked questions

My purchase did not show up.

Open the Trading Post and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. If it still does not appear, force quit the app, reopen it, and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

Do I own a world forever once I buy it?

Yes. Every world unlock is a one time purchase tied to your Apple Account. It never expires, and you can restore it on any device you sign in to.

Can I finish the game without paying?

Yes. Eleven worlds and the daily puzzle are free, which is more than a thousand levels. Purchases add optional worlds and coins, and they are never required to finish what you already have.

How do the daily puzzle and streaks work?

A new puzzle arrives each day. Solving it keeps your streak alive, and you earn a Rest Day at a three day streak so one missed morning does not undo your progress.

How do friend codes work?

Tap Invite Friends for your six character code. When a friend enters it, they receive a welcome bonus and you receive coins the next time you open the game. Codes are matched through iCloud, so both players need to be signed in to iCloud.

What are Traveler Cards?

Premium worlds award a collectible card every fifth level, twenty per world. Finish a level flawlessly and the card is gold. Completing a full set pays a grand prize.

How do I turn the music off?

Tap the music note in the top corner of the home screen. The app also follows your ring and silent switch, and it mixes under music you are already playing.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync in this version, so we cannot see your progress or send it back to you. It is lost if you delete the app, and it does not carry over to a new phone unless you restore that phone from a backup of the old one. If you restore your phone from an iCloud or encrypted computer backup, your progress comes back with it.

Your purchases are different. Every world unlock is tied to your Apple Account, so open the Trading Post and tap Restore purchases and they return on any device. If your progress vanished without deleting the app, please write to us with your device model and iOS version. We cannot rebuild your progress, but we will find the cause and fix it for everyone.
